## Observed Comparison

### Identity

- Radix creates a visible, stable chromatic rail in both appearances. It gives
  the monochrome N mark a service-owned anchor while leaving jackets and data
  surfaces neutral.
- Polaris preserves a fully achromatic shell. Its rail aligns with the current
  monochrome mark but contributes little additional recognition; typography,
  spacing, placement, and the mark must carry identity.
- `EXP-01` creates the Polaris achromatic shell in Light and the Radix chromatic
  anchor in Dark. It remains restrained inside each appearance, but the remembered
  color identity changes when the theme changes.
- All three keep content more prominent than the signature cue under the approved
  sparse-use boundary.

### Rare action candidate

- Radix produces a clear but bounded primary emphasis in both appearances.
- Polaris is not automatically quieter. Its Dark published brand fill is white
  on `#111111`, making the action substantially more salient than Radix.
- `EXP-01` uses Polaris's strong charcoal action in Light and Radix's lower-luminance
  chromatic action in Dark, avoiding the Polaris Dark white-fill problem. The same
  semantic action instead moves between achromatic and indigo treatments.
- Therefore source selection and action eligibility must remain separate. A
  user may advance Polaris for identity while rejecting its white Dark filled
  action, leaving the action neutral; the same separate gate applies to Radix.

### Domain and content coexistence

- Actual jackets remain the strongest content color under all three candidates.
- Radix is perceptually close to Discord, Real, and Basic evidence. Restricted
  area and semantic placement keep the specimen readable, but broad use would
  fail the approved color budget.
- Polaris has the lowest hue collision, but a filled Dark action can compete
  with the chart and dense record hierarchy through luminance rather than hue.
- `EXP-01` reduces hue collision in Light and white-fill competition in Dark, but
  retains Radix's Dark collision cost against the domain evidence.
- All three leave left/right chart notes readable because no candidate enters
  the renderer or hand legend.

### Appearance continuity

- Identity-rail placement, mark, label, action hierarchy, and non-color cues remain
  the same across appearances.
- `EXP-01` does not break technical theme parity, but it has weaker continuity than
  the two full-source candidates if users must remember NosLog as one color.
- The experiment therefore asks whether appearance-specific restraint should take
  priority over one continuous Light/Dark color identity.

## Selection Outcome and Remaining Gate

1. **Identity source — Approved:** intact `SS-08` Radix Colors Indigo. The exact
   published Light/Dark mapping remains one indivisible source set.
2. **Alternatives — Not selected:** Shopify Polaris and `EXP-01`. Their evidence is
   retained and may be revisited only if the approved source fails a later measured
   requirement.
3. **Rare action eligibility — Pending:** decide separately whether the exact Radix
   filled-action mapping may be used for a proven rare primary action or actions
   remain neutral.

The selection preserves the `SC-B` single-source contract. It does not authorize
recoloring ordinary links, filters, selected containers, domain values, focus, or the
monochrome N mark.
