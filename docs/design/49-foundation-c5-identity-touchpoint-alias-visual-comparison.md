## Fixed Comparison Contract

1. Adobe Spectrum S2 supplies every Light/Dark neutral surface, foreground, and
   boundary in the candidate fragments.
2. Radix Colors Indigo remains the approved identity source. The comparison uses
   exact default `#3E63DD`; it does not alter hue, saturation, or appearance value.
3. Fluent `FI-C` remains Light black / Dark white, `2px`, zero-gap focus. Default
   identity marks have no white outline.
4. Candidate color may appear only in the graphical mark or its field. It does
   not recolor the wordmark, page title, link, action, selected state, chart data,
   or feedback.
5. All candidates use the same provisional filled geometric `N` silhouette. It is
   comparison scaffolding, not the final NosLog logo.
6. `ITA-B` uses a square field with zero radius, border, and shadow to avoid
   silently selecting Foundation geometry. The square is not a proposed final
   app icon.
7. `FCM-11`, `SIG-07`, the full Indigo header, the Indigo small-text wordmark, and
   the thin identity rail are not rendered.

## Observed Visual Differences

### `ITA-A` — Indigo mark

- Produces the smallest persistent chromatic area and leaves the wordmark and
  shell hierarchy almost entirely neutral.
- The uncontained mark remains clear on Light canvas and Dark canvas, but is the
  least assertive colored option at compact header size.
- In authentication and recovery contexts it reads as a colored symbol followed
  by an independently legible service name.
- Its viability depends on the later final mark having enough filled area and
  distinctiveness. A thin final drawing would not inherit this specimen's measured
  non-text behavior automatically.

### `ITA-B` — Indigo field

- Produces the strongest and most theme-stable service recognition of the three
  while keeping chromatic area bounded to one compact field.
- White-on-Indigo remains legible in all four contexts without using a Dark white
  outline or changing the approved identity value.
- The field distinguishes the mark more clearly from chart and content text than
  `ITA-A`.
- It can resemble a generic app tile if field geometry is treated as decoration.
  Selection of `ITA-B` would therefore open, not settle, the field-shape and radius
  gate.

### `ITA-C` — Achromatic control

- Preserves the quietest content hierarchy and has no hue collision with domain
  or external-brand colors.
- Stable placement and the visible wordmark still identify NosLog in every context.
- It does not visibly exercise the approved Radix identity source in the shell and
  has the weakest cross-context chromatic recognition.
- It remains a valid result if the user decides the service mark should stay
  achromatic and Radix should be reserved for another later-approved identity role.

## Approved Outcome

The specimen narrowed the choice to one material tradeoff:

- choose `ITA-A` when the smallest possible color area is more important than the
  strongest small-size recognition;
- choose `ITA-B` when one fixed, compact, single-color field is desirable across
  Light and Dark, accepting a later field-geometry gate;
- choose `ITA-C` when content neutrality is more important than a visible chromatic
  shell identity.

The user selected `ITA-C`. NosLog shell identity must rely on stable placement,
the final mark silhouette, the visible wordmark, typography, spacing, and semantics
rather than coloring the logo. The approved mapping is:

- graphical mark: adaptive Spectrum S2 neutral foreground;
- visible `NosLog` wordmark: adaptive Spectrum S2 neutral foreground;
- mark field: transparent;
- default outline: none;
- actual keyboard focus: approved Fluent `FI-C` only.

`ITA-A` and `ITA-B` are rejected chromatic logo treatments. Their measured evidence
remains in this document and specimen so the decision history is not discarded.
Radix Indigo remains the approved signature identity source, but it has no approved
shell identity alias. Any different bounded placement requires a separate user-approved
gate; this decision does not silently move Indigo into actions or content.
